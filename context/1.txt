To all DEPARTMENTAL CHAIRMEN regarding 
Two-Day Training Programme for Male Lecturers
,.........
Venue: Bangladesh Academy for Rural Development (BARD), Cumilla
Date: 20–21 August 2026
Departure: 19 August 2026 at 4:00 PM from IIUC Kumira Campus

All Departmental Chairmen are requested to ensure strict compliance with the following guidelines regarding the participation of their respective lecturers in the two-day training programme:

1. Mandatory Participation
Participation in the training programme is mandatory for all male lecturers.
Departmental Chairmen shall ensure that all concerned lecturers attend the programme without exception.
Any unavoidable absence must be reported to the appropriate authority in advance with a valid reason.
2. Departure from Campus
All participating lecturers shall assemble at the designated point at the IIUC Kumira Campus by 3:45 PM on 19 August 2026.
The group will leave the campus for Cumilla at exactly 4:00 PM.
Departmental Chairmen are requested to ensure that no participant is delayed due to departmental or personal reasons.
3. Preparation for the Stay
Participants shall make necessary personal arrangements for two nights/three days, including appropriate clothing, toiletries, medicines if required, academic materials, and other essentials.
Participants are advised to carry their university ID cards and any materials/instruments specifically required for the training.
4. Punctuality
All participants must strictly follow the schedule provided by the training authority.
Participants shall attend every session, workshop, group activity and other scheduled programme on time.
Late arrival at any session should be avoided.
5. Attendance and Full Participation
Participants are expected to remain present throughout the entire training programme.
Mere physical presence will not be considered sufficient; participants are expected to actively participate in discussions, workshops, group activities and other learning exercises.
6. Final Assessment
An evaluation examination/assessment will be conducted on the final day of the training.
Participation in the assessment is mandatory for all trainees.
Departmental Chairmen shall ensure that all participants remain available until the completion of the assessment and other concluding activities.
7. Discipline and Professional Conduct
All participants shall maintain the highest standards of discipline, courtesy, professionalism and institutional dignity throughout the programme.
Participants must comply with the rules and regulations of BARD and the instructions of the programme authorities.
Any behaviour that may adversely affect the reputation of the University shall be strictly avoided.
8. Mobile Phone Usage
Mobile phones shall remain switched off or kept on silent mode during lectures, discussions, workshops, presentations and other academic sessions.
Unnecessary use of mobile phones, social media or other electronic devices during sessions is strictly discouraged.
9. Compliance with Group Leaders
Participants shall strictly follow the instructions and guidance of their respective Group Leaders/Group Heads.
Group Leaders shall coordinate attendance, movement, participation and other group-related activities.
Participants are expected to cooperate fully with their Group Leaders.
10. Dress and Personal Appearance
Participants shall maintain decent, formal and professional attire throughout the training programme.
Appropriate dress should be maintained during all official sessions, presentations and institutional visits.
11. Active Learning and Teamwork
Participants are encouraged to contribute meaningfully to discussions, case studies, workshops, presentations and group assignments.
They should demonstrate a positive attitude towards learning, teamwork, communication and professional development.
12. Respectful Communication
Participants shall maintain respectful and courteous communication with trainers, BARD officials, colleagues, group leaders and other participants.
Arguments, inappropriate comments, disruptive behaviour or any form of misconduct must be avoided.
13. Institutional Representation
Every participant should remember that he/she is representing International Islamic University Chittagong (IIUC).
Participants are therefore expected to uphold the University's values, reputation, discipline and professional standards at all times.
14. Academic and Administrative Responsibility
Departmental Chairmen should make necessary arrangements so that participation in the training does not create avoidable disruption to departmental academic activities.
Any pending departmental responsibility of the participating lecturers should be appropriately managed before departure.
15. Health, Safety and Personal Responsibility
Participants shall take reasonable care of their personal health, belongings and safety during the journey and stay.
Any health-related or emergency matter should immediately be communicated to the designated coordinator/group leader.
16. Coordination with the Organising Authority
Departmental Chairmen shall maintain necessary communication with the designated university coordinator regarding attendance, transportation, accommodation and other programme-related matters.
Any problem or unexpected situation should be communicated promptly to the appropriate authority.
17. Return Journey
Participants shall remain with their respective groups until the official completion of the programme.
The return journey will be undertaken according to the schedule communicated by the University/organising authority.
18. Overall Responsibility of Departmental Chairmen

Departmental Chairmen are requested to brief the concerned lecturers about these guidelines before their departure and ensure their full cooperation and compliance throughout the programme.

The success of this training programme will largely depend on discipline, punctuality, active participation, teamwork and professional conduct. All concerned are therefore requested to extend their fullest cooperation to make the programme meaningful, successful and worthy of the University's objectives.
