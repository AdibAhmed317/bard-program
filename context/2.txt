Subject: Mandatory Participation in Training Program at BARD, Cumilla

As directed, this is to inform all concerned that a training program will be held on 20–21 August 2026 at the Bangladesh Academy for Rural Development (BARD), Cumilla.

All male lecturers are hereby instructed to participate in the training program. The participants will depart from the University campus for Cumilla on 19 August 2026 at 4:00 PM.

Participation in the training program is mandatory for all concerned lecturers. All participants are therefore requested to make the necessary preparations and ensure their presence and punctual participation throughout the program.

The necessary guidelines and other relevant instructions regarding the training program may be obtained from the respective Departmental Chairmen and the Director, CGED.

All concerned are requested to comply with the above instructions strictly.

Registrar, IIUC
